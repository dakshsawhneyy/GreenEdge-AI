# Lighting Energy Efficiency

Lighting systems can consume unnecessary electricity
when they operate without regard to occupancy.

## Occupancy-Based Controls

Occupancy sensors can switch or dim lighting based
on whether a space is being used.

## Daylight Utilization

Daylight-responsive controls can reduce artificial
lighting when sufficient natural light is available.

## Monitoring

Building operators should compare lighting schedules
with actual occupancy patterns and investigate
unnecessary operating hours.
