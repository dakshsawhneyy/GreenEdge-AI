# HVAC Energy Efficiency

HVAC systems are significant consumers of energy in
commercial buildings.

## Temperature Setpoint Optimization

Unnecessarily low cooling setpoints can increase
cooling demand. Building operators should review
temperature setpoints against occupancy, comfort
requirements, and applicable building standards.

## Airflow Management

Poor airflow distribution can create inefficient
cooling patterns. Operators should inspect air
distribution, filters, dampers, and cooling equipment.

## Scheduling

HVAC equipment should operate according to actual
building occupancy and operational requirements.
Running cooling systems at full capacity in
unoccupied spaces may waste energy.

## Monitoring

Operators should monitor temperature, power
consumption, occupancy, and cooling-system
performance to identify abnormal operating patterns.
